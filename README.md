## 📖 The Backstory

We’ve all been there: you're stuck in a mandatory computer lab class, the internet is down (or blocked), the professor is droning on, and you need a stealthy way to keep your brain alive without raising suspicion. 

If you alt-tab to a browser game, you're dead. But a black terminal window full of text? To any teacher walking past, you look like a hardcore systems programmer grinding out assignments. In reality, you're just trying to figure out why the computer keeps picking 73.

Built purely in C++, this lightweight command-line game was forged out of sheer class boredom.
